# Application Document: Food Donation & Redistribution Platform

##  App Name (Placeholder)

**OneBowl**
*(“One platform to share food with humans and animals”)*

---

#  1. What This Application Is About

This application is a **smart food redistribution platform** that connects:

*  Restaurants with leftover food
*  Households with excess homemade food
*  Events (weddings, parties, corporate functions)
*  NGOs and shelters
*  Animal feeders and rescue groups
*  Pickup volunteers/drivers

###  Core Purpose:

To **reduce food waste and fight hunger**, while also supporting **animal feeding and welfare**, through a **single, organized digital system**.

---

#  2. Problem It Solves

Every day:

* Tons of edible food is wasted from restaurants and events
* Homes discard surplus food after celebrations
* NGOs struggle with unpredictable food supply
* Stray animals remain unfed despite available surplus food nearby

###  Current Gap:

Existing solutions are:

* Fragmented (only restaurants OR NGOs)
* Not real-time
* Not optimized for logistics
* Not inclusive of animal feeding

---

#  3. Solution Overview

The app acts as a **real-time food redistribution network**:

. Users donate food
. System matches nearby receivers (NGOs or animal feeders)
. Pickup agents collect food
. Food is delivered efficiently and transparently

Everything happens in **one streamlined platform**.

#  5. How the App Works (Step-by-Step Flow)

##  Step 1: Donation Creation

A donor opens the app and:

* Selects food type (veg / non-veg / animal-safe)
* Enters quantity
* Uploads photo
* Sets pickup time & location

---

##  Step 2: Smart Matching System

The system automatically:

* Finds nearest NGOs or animal feeders
* Checks food suitability
* Assigns pickup agent

---

##  Step 3: Pickup Process

* Pickup agent receives notification
* Navigates to donor location
* Verifies food (photo + checklist)
* Collects food

---

##  Step 4: Delivery

* Food delivered to assigned NGO or feeder
* Receiver confirms delivery
* System updates status

---

##  Step 5: Impact Tracking

* Donor receives:

  * Delivery confirmation
  * Impact stats (meals served / animals fed)
  * Photos (optional proof)
